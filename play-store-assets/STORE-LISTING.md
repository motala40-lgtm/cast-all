## Full Description (max 4000 characters)

### English

Easy Cast turns your phone into a simple remote for your TV. Cast local videos, photos, and music straight from your gallery — no cables, no complicated setup.

**Works with almost any smart TV**
Easy Cast supports both Google Cast (Chromecast, Chromecast built-in, Android TV, Google TV) and DLNA — so it also works with many Samsung and other smart TVs that don't have Chromecast built in.

**What you can cast**
• Local videos, photos, and music straight from your phone's gallery
• Any direct video URL
• A Playlist — pick multiple photos and videos at once and they'll play through automatically, with the option to add background music too

**Playback controls, right from your phone**
• Play / pause, skip ±10 seconds, and a real seek bar
• Volume control synced with the TV
• A sleep timer to auto-pause after 15, 30, or 60 minutes

**Make it yours**
• Light, dark, or system theme — or pick your own photo as the app's background
• A set of accent colors to match the whole app to your taste
• Available in English, Persian, Arabic, Turkish, Russian, Chinese, German, French, Spanish, and Swedish

**Privacy-friendly**
Easy Cast only talks to devices on your own local Wi-Fi network to cast media you choose. It doesn't collect or share your personal data.

Questions or feedback? Reach out any time — see the Support option inside the app's Settings.
